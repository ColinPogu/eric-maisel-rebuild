About Eric

A humane approach to being human

Eric Maisel has spent more than forty years as an author, family therapist, creativity coach, teacher, and trainer, and as an advocate for more humane ways of meeting human difficulty.

His work asks practical questions. How do we create meaning when it is not simply given to us? How do we live creatively? How can we understand our minds without rushing to a label, and help others without pretending to have every answer? These questions run through his books, his coaching, and his trainings.

A way of helping that accompanies rather than diagnoses, and collaborates rather than prescribes. The Human Experience Specialist
